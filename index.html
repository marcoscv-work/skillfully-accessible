How it works

Every skill made here stacks three layers. When two layers disagree, the one on top wins: your own patterns come before everything else.

  • Your patterns

    How your team solves each case, plus links to your documentation. The AI follows them exactly, so every screen is consistent.

    You write these

  • Your weak spots

    The criteria your audits keep failing. The skill puts them first and checks every one before it finishes.

    You choose these

  • The base

    All of WCAG 2.2 Level AA: 55 success criteria, each with what to check in code.

    Always included

Build a skill

Three short steps. Nothing leaves your browser.

1 About your team

Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. It names the skill's folder.

2 Your weak spots

Pick the success criteria your audits fail most. You can leave this empty.

For example: 1.4.3, 2.4.7 and 4.1.2. Any text works; numbers are picked out.

No weak spots selected.

3 Your documentation

One per line. How your team solves a case, said as a rule.

One per line, as Title | URL.

How to install it

Save the file as in your project, or in ~/.claude/skills to use it everywhere. Claude Code picks it up the next time it starts. Other assistants that read SKILL.md files work the same way.
